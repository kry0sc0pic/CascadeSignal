# FTX collapse (November 2022)

- **Window analyzed:** 2022-11-06 → 2022-11-11
- **Published scale (PLAN §2):** "Elevated liquidations; emergency market
  pauses" — no single published USD/count figure exists for this episode
  (unlike China/Oct'25/Feb'26), consistent with the mechanism below.
- **Observed in our data (Aave v2, same window):** $20.9M / 324 liquidation
  events

## Mechanism

FTX's collapse was predominantly a **CeFi contagion event** — a
counterparty/exchange solvency crisis, not a DeFi-native price or oracle
shock. Liquidity and confidence stress propagated through CeFi lenders and
funds, but Aave v2 positions were comparatively insulated: collateral prices
moved, but not with the sharp, concentrated intensity of China or Terra.

## Detection status: ❌ Not detected (documented finding, not a data gap)

D-A does not label this window as a cascade at any of the 27 grid points —
confirmed even pooling all 5 Mock-1 protocols (Aave v2/v3, Compound v2/v3,
Maker; see `docs/decisions/ADR-001-cascade-definition.md` §Findings). Max
liquidated USD in any single 100-block window during this period is ~$7.6M,
well under the ~$30M p99.5 severity threshold set by China/Terra/Oct'25/
Feb'26.

**This is treated as a real finding, not a defect.** Per ADR-001's
pre-registration discipline, D-A's thresholds were not loosened after the
fact to force this episode to clear — that would be exactly the kind of
post-hoc tuning pre-registration exists to prevent. Revisit via D-B (Hawkes
declustering, CAS-17) or D-C (POT), which key off intensity/rate rather
than a fixed severity bar and may be more sensitive to a sustained-but-
lower-amplitude pattern like this one.

## Data coverage

Aave v2 mainnet liquidations, complete for this window. No mempool coverage
(pre-Aug-2023).
